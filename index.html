EvolveMe ("EvolveMe", "we", "us", or "our") is a personal health and fitness application designed to help users understand their activity, workouts, recovery, and health-related information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information EvolveMe may access, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

Important: EvolveMe is a wellness and fitness application. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

1Information we collect and access

Health and fitness data

With your permission, EvolveMe may access health and fitness information through Apple HealthKit, including information such as:

  • Steps and activity information
  • Active energy and calorie information
  • Walking and running distance
  • Heart rate
  • Resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Sleep information
  • Workout information
  • Other HealthKit data that you explicitly authorize

EvolveMe only accesses HealthKit information for which you grant permission. You can manage or revoke HealthKit permissions at any time through Apple's Health settings.

Workout data

When you record workouts using EvolveMe or the EvolveMe Apple Watch app, we may process information associated with the workout, including workout type, duration, heart rate, calories, and other workout metrics made available by Apple HealthKit.

Apple Watch data

If you use the EvolveMe Apple Watch app, workout information may be exchanged between your iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This enables workout controls, workout status, and relevant workout metrics to remain synchronized between your devices.

Information you provide

EvolveMe may process information that you voluntarily enter into the application, such as workout selections, goals, preferences, and information provided when interacting with EvolveMe features.

Technical information

Certain technical information may be processed by the services used to operate EvolveMe, such as application configuration, device-related information, diagnostic information, and information necessary to maintain security and reliability.

2How we use information

EvolveMe uses information for purposes including:

  • Displaying your health and fitness information
  • Recording and displaying workouts
  • Tracking activity and recovery trends
  • Providing personalized wellness insights
  • Supporting EvolveMe AI features
  • Synchronizing supported iPhone and Apple Watch features
  • Improving application reliability and functionality
  • Protecting the application against abuse and unauthorized use

3Apple HealthKit

EvolveMe uses Apple HealthKit to provide health and fitness functionality.

HealthKit information is accessed only after you grant the appropriate permission. You remain in control of HealthKit permissions through Apple's Health application and device settings.

EvolveMe does not use HealthKit data for advertising. EvolveMe does not sell HealthKit data.

HealthKit data is not used for advertising, marketing, or other purposes prohibited by Apple's HealthKit policies.

4AI features

EvolveMe may provide artificial intelligence features that generate wellness-oriented responses, summaries, recommendations, or other informational output.

Depending on the feature you use, relevant information may be processed by third-party technology providers that support EvolveMe's AI functionality.

We aim to limit information sent to AI services to information necessary to provide the requested feature.

AI-generated information is provided for general wellness and informational purposes and should not be treated as medical advice.

5Firebase and security services

EvolveMe uses Firebase and related services to support application functionality, security, authentication or backend functionality, where applicable.

EvolveMe may use Firebase App Check to help verify that requests originate from legitimate instances of the application and to protect backend services from abuse.

Information processed by these services is subject to the applicable policies and security practices of the service providers.

6Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may use service providers that process information on our behalf when necessary to operate EvolveMe, provide AI functionality, maintain security, or provide backend infrastructure.

We do not share your health information with advertisers.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the security, rights, or property of EvolveMe, our users, or others.

7Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant EvolveMe functionality, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

Information stored directly on your Apple devices may remain on those devices according to Apple's operating system and HealthKit behavior until you delete it or revoke access.

8Your choices and controls

You can control EvolveMe's access to HealthKit through the Apple Health application and your device settings.

You may also stop using EvolveMe and delete the application from your device.

If you have a privacy-related request concerning information processed by EvolveMe, you may contact us using the contact information below.

9Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed by EvolveMe against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10Children's privacy

EvolveMe is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use the application under applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

11Third-party services

EvolveMe may depend on third-party platforms and services, including Apple services and Firebase-related infrastructure. These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective functionality.

Your use of Apple services is also subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

12Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to EvolveMe, applicable law, or our privacy practices.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the beginning of this policy.

13Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy requests, or questions about how EvolveMe handles information, please contact us.
